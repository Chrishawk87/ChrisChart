READ THIS PART
========================================================================
  Section 1 decides whether any of the rest matters. If the
  strongest correlation sits at zero lag or at a positive one,
  the book is not leading price on this data and no amount of
  regime slicing in sections 2-4 will change that.

  Every rate in sections 2 and 4 is against the base rate. A
  number that merely matches it is a finding of nothing, which
  is a real result and worth knowing in an afternoon rather
  than after a month of live trading.
