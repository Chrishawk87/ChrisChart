HOW TO READ IT
========================================================================
  Compare every row to the CONTROL on the same horizon, never
  to 50%. A state matching the control has added nothing.

  THE PAIRS ARE THE TEST.
    permanent_ask and permanent_bid are the same event on
    opposite sides. A real effect appears in BOTH, with the
    same sign once each is read in its own direction. One
    alone is this sample's drift wearing the event's name.

    The same for failed_ask and failed_bid -- AND the two
    pairs must point OPPOSITE ways. If a cleared offer and a
    defended offer both precede the same direction, the
    reading is not about defence at all.

  MEAN MATTERS MORE THAN up%. ES costs a tick to cross. A
  state that is 55% up with a mean of 0.05 ticks is a losing
  trade taken often. Read the mean against 1.0.
